    Why it isn't playing

    
  
    

  
    
    
    Earshot plays through YouTube's own embedded player with the video surface hidden, so you get the highest-quality audio stream YouTube serves for that upload — adaptive AAC or Opus, typically 128–160 kbps. Nothing is downloaded, re-encoded, or stored.
    
    
    A few uploads have embedding switched off by their owner; those report an error here and have to be opened on YouTube. If a track loads but stays silent, hit Show picture and press play once — some browsers demand a click on the player itself before they will let sound through.
